About Teto 💕

Kasane Teto (重音テト) is a popular Japanese virtual singer initially created on April 1, 2008, as an April Fools' Day hoax to troll fans by mimicking a new Vocaloid. Originally a fan-made UTAU character, her popularity led to an official Synthesizer V AI voice database in 2023. Teto is characterized as a cheerful, 31-year-old "chimera" (half-human, half-machine) with a tsundere personality who loves French bread and is easily recognized by her red hair, twin-drills, and, often, a baguette.
